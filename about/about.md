<h1> This is the 'about' page </h1>

<p>My name is Jenn Wilson. I was born in Adelaide, Australia which is <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaurna">Kaurna country</a>, and I currently live in Melbourne, Australia on the lands of the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bunurong">Bunurong people</a>. I live with two cats (one with white and black markings, the other is a calico) and one husband (blonde). My own hair oscillates between teal and pink, with some amount of personal effort each time to alter it. My favourite shape is a rhombus, and has been since childhood.</p>

<p>I'm in the final stages of my PhD, which I am doing at <a href="https://www.monash.edu/arts/media-film-journalism">Monash University</a>, where I sit within the Faculty of Arts. I am also affiliated with the <a href="https://www.admscentre.org.au/jenn-wilson/">Centre for Automated Decision Making and Society</a>. You can read about my <a href="/projects/phd.html">PhD</a>, where I used large language models (two open-weight, one proprietary) to produce explanatory text about several pieces of federal Australian Parliamentary legislation. My thesis discusses hallucination, omission, and emotion in the outputs of the models. </p>

<p>I am the kind of person who has side projects. I have two additional projects presently: <a href="/projects/dolly.html">one on Dolly magazine</a> and one on <a href="/projects/rental.html">rental legislation</a>.
